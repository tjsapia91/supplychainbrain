# (C) PO Approval Workflow — Harry / Ningbo Zeyu (and vendor POs generally)

The lifecycle a PO goes through from issue to "live." Used to drive the **[[01 Purchasing & Inventory/(C) PO Tracker — Harry (Ningbo Zeyu)]]** status column.

## The 7 steps

| # | Step | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **PPO → Harry** | Tom → Harry | Tom emails the PPO to Harry |
| 2 | **PI ← Harry** | Harry → Tom | Harry returns the Proforma Invoice (PI) |
| 3 | **Review / adjust** | Tom (↔ Harry) | Tom checks the PI; if changes needed, loop with Harry until correct |
| 4 | **→ Approvers** | Tom → Lilia, Donna, Supply Chain | Tom sends the **PO + PI** to Lilia, Donna, and Supply Chain |
| 5 | **Approved → Michael** | Lilia **or** Donna | Approver emails **Michael** (Tom cc'd) saying "these are good to sign" |
| 6 | **Signed ← Michael** | Michael → Tom | Michael (CEO) signs and emails the signed copy back to Tom |
| 7 | **Signed → Harry** | Tom → Harry | Tom emails the signed copy to Harry → **PO is LIVE** |

## Key point
- **The authoritative "signed" = MICHAEL's signature** (step 6), not Tom's. When Tom emails Harry "approved and signed PO's" (step 7), that copy has already been through Michael.
- Steps 3 can loop (PI adjustments) before moving to approval.
